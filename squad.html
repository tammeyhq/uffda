What happens at a meetup

Challenges, support & real progress

Each month the Squad gets together to share challenges, tips and tools, and to connect with people who are looking for support and willing to offer it.

  • Bring a task you've been putting off. Saying it out loud is half the battle.
  • Brainstorm with people who understand the difficulties of being a solopreneur.
  • Leave with a next step and someone who'll ask how it went.

Meetups are the 2nd Friday of each month. Time and location are posted in the Squad's Facebook group, so follow along there to be the first to know.

Learn something new. Do something amazing. Technology, creativity, and practical skills made easier, one step at a time.
