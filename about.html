It started over coffee

Sue and Tammey at Red River Coffee during one of their weekly board meetings.

It started over coffee in 2017. Tammey and Sue began scheduling weekly "board meetings," time blocked on their calendars to meet, connect, share what was going on in their lives, and brainstorm. They called them board meetings because seeing them on the calendar reminded them of the importance of their time together.

Along the way, they started helping each other with the tasks that felt impossible. One week Sue had a client task that felt overwhelming, so Tammey simply did it for her. A few months later Tammey had one of her own, and Sue took it on. Done and done!

Then one day the conversation turned to what they could bring to their community. In the summer of 2022, Uffda Academy was born.

Meet Tammey

I'm a problem solver, technology enthusiast, and teacher at heart with over 30 years of experience in business systems, organization, training, and finding better ways to get things done.

I love turning complicated processes into practical solutions, whether that means building websites, improving workflows, exploring new technology, or helping someone master a new skill.

That passion inspired me to co-create Uffda Academy.

I believe technology should make life easier, not more complicated. My favorite moments are when someone goes from "I have no idea how to do this" to "Look what I just did!"

A little patience, a little humor, and a willingness to try can take us a long way!
