Privacy Policy

Uffda Academy respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through uffdaacademy.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

This website doesn't require an account and doesn't collect personal information unless you choose to send it to us, for example by emailing us or signing up for updates.

The Social Media Type Quiz runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and score are not sent to us or stored.

How we use your information

If you email us or sign up for updates, we use your information only to respond to you and to send the updates you asked for. We don't sell or rent your information.

Third-party services

Our website is hosted by a third-party provider that may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and browser type) for security and performance. Links to other websites, such as Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Google Calendar, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
